A FEW ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions.

What you can try in this portfolio demo.

Can I buy a belt here?+

No. This is a portfolio demo. Reviewing an order shows a sample summary without payment or order fulfilment.

How is the total calculated?+

The original coursework price is $78.95 per belt. The selected delivery option adds $0, $1 or $5.

Are the account pages real?+

The original ASP.NET source implements registration and login. This static demo has a temporary member mode and does not create genuine accounts.

Does the contact form send an email?+

No. Both the original contact handler and this demo display an on-page confirmation. This demo sends no message.

What are the shipping and return policies?+

The policy pages show coursework examples. They are not an offer of sale or a real shipping or returns service.
